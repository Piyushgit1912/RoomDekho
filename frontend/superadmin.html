Operations Strategy

Current Operational Flow: Direct Listing

To ensure the fastest possible onboarding and property listing process for owners, the automated verification queue has been bypassed. All new property uploads and updates immediately go live on the listings platform under Approved status. Superadmin verification is handled post-listing or through report moderation.
