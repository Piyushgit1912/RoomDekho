About Us

Helping Indians Find
Home Away From Home

RoomZey connects students and working professionals with verified, affordable accommodation — without any broker fees.

Our Mission

Finding a room when you move to a new city for college or work is one of the most stressful experiences for young Indians. Brokers overcharge, listings are fake, and the process is opaque.

RoomZey was built to fix that. We provide a free, verified, and transparent marketplace where property owners and tenants connect directly.
